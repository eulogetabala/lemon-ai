# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audiences (confirmed): créateurs et solopreneurs, freelances et agences, équipes marketing. They publish across multiple social channels and need one place to plan, draft, adapt, and schedule content without tab chaos.

## Product Purpose

Lemon.ai centralizes ideas, drafts, calendar, and multi-channel publishing with an integrated AI assistant, so users ship consistent content faster.

## Positioning

One workspace that turns a global draft into channel-ready posts, schedules them, and publishes automatically — with AI for ideas and rewriting inside the same flow.

## Operating Context

Creators and teams work in browser sessions: connect channels, organize ideas (Kanban), compose posts with per-channel previews, schedule via calendar/list views, rely on cron-backed publishing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: Clerk auth; multi-channel connect (X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, Facebook, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok); AI generate/shorten/rephrase/expand; calendar & list; Kanban ideas; automated scheduling. Pricing displayed in FCFA. Logo mark and name Lemon.ai must remain unchanged. Visual world is open for full redesign (user constraint: no gradients).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lemon.ai
- Logo component and wordmark structure preserved
- French UI copy for marketing surfaces
- Binding visual constraint from user: no gradients; creative layout freedom

## Evidence on Hand

Marketing copy and plan feature lists live on the landing page. No third-party testimonials, customer logos, or verified benchmarks on hand — do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Prove the scheduling mechanism; do not only claim productivity.
2. Keep multi-audience relevance without diluting the creator-first entry path.
3. Preserve product truth (channels, plans, capabilities) while replacing visual identity.
4. Prefer clarity and distinctive composition over decorative chrome.
